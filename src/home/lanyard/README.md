# Lanyard images

Replace these files (keep the names; png, jpg, jpeg or webp) and refresh:

- `card-front.png` — front of the badge, 1024 x 1440 px
- `card-back.png` — back of the badge, 1024 x 1440 px
- `strap.png` — pattern tiled along the strap (any small image; it repeats)

`card-template.png` is a blank with guides (card edge, safe area, the
area the metal clip covers) — open it in your design tool and export your
design over the top. It isn't loaded by the site.
